Privacy

Last updated 17 October 2026

In short

No cookies, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking. The Calorie Tracker is for a small group of invited users. What you log about your eating, your weight and your goals is stored on our own server in Switzerland, is only visible to you (and to people you send a share link to), and is never sold or passed on.

Who is responsible

Pio Gino Steiner, Switzerland · info@piogino.ch
The Calorie Tracker is a private, non-commercial hobby project.

What we store for your account

  • username, email address and password (stored only as a secure hash), your role and daily calorie goal
  • the foods and meals you log, with amounts, calories, times and day comments
  • weight entries, goals, streaks, points and rewards
  • meal photos you upload, or the address of a photo you link
  • if you turn on reminders: the push subscription of your browser

Food, weight and goal entries can say something about your health, which Swiss law treats as sensitive personal data. We process them only to show them back to you; nobody else can see them unless you share them. You provide them voluntarily, and you can delete entries in the app at any time.

How long we keep it

Your account and entries are kept until you ask us to delete them. Uploaded photos are deleted automatically after 300 days. Share links stop working after 30 days at the latest.

Sharing

You can create a share link for chosen days. Anyone with that link can see the meals of those days, without signing in, until the link expires or you revoke it. Only send it to people you trust.

Stored in your browser

The app sets no cookies. It keeps these values in your browser's local storage:

  • authToken: your sign-in, valid for 24 hours, so you stay logged in
  • appState, favoriteFoods, foodSearchCache: a copy of your current entries, favourite foods and recent searches, so the app is fast and works offline
  • calorie-tracker-theme, searchPreferences, databaseTogglePreferences: display and search settings

They stay on your device, apart from the sign-in, which is sent with each request to our server. Logging out removes the sign-in; clearing the site data in your browser's settings removes everything.

Data abroad

Your account and entries stay in Switzerland. Loading the app reaches GitHub in the USA; GitHub states that it participates in the Swiss-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. Open Food Facts is in France, which Switzerland recognises as providing adequate data protection. Push services are run by the browser makers, mostly from the USA.

Your rights

Under the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) you can ask what personal data about you is processed, get a copy of it, and have it corrected or deleted, including your whole account. Write to info@piogino.ch. You can also contact the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC).
